# Bunz narrative pattern

Source: Michael B. Wong (HKU), *Money and Barter in the Field: Evidence from the Life and Death of a Digital Currency*, Society of Economic Dynamics Annual Meeting, June 2025. The user supplied Bunz_slides.pdf. There are 33 logical main slides; the 69-page PDF includes animation stages and an appendix. This guide paraphrases the structure; it does not redistribute the PDF.

| Logical slides | Narrative function | Transferable decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2--5 | Motivation, This paper, Main findings, Related literature | Establish question, research leverage, results, and contribution early. |
| 7--8 | Minimal search framework and predictions | Explain money supply and redemption through distinct outcome predictions. |
| 10--14 | Community, trading mechanics, token rules, data | Show how the model's frictions and variables appear in observed transactions. |
| 16--18 | Monetary expansion | Establish the supply change, show trading responses and unchanged barter, then regression evidence. |
| 20--24 | Reduced redemption | Examine supply and prices before acceptance and trading to distinguish explanations. |
| 26--30 | Redemption halt | Repeat a familiar evidence structure for a new event and compare responses. |
| 32--33 | Discussion and contributions | Explain both model fit and deviations, then state evidence-grounded contributions. |

Do not copy Bunz's three events, causal claims, exact slide count, or appendix into another study. Preserve its discipline of matching theory to observable responses. Its informative unchanged variables are part of the argument, not expendable nonresults.

## Adaptation exercise

For an observational AI prediction-market study, a suitable sequence is title -> motivating paradox -> question and This paper -> main findings -> literature/contribution -> shared-information framework -> setting/data -> adoption evidence -> collective forecasting evidence -> return-distribution evidence -> discussion/contributions.

The three empirical margins may use different samples and concentration measures. They do not automatically identify a single equilibrium mechanism. A same-tool label does not prove shared signal realizations. A short-term adoption effect with an insignificant later horizon should not become a claim of persistent adoption. A consensus result that depends on the aggregation rule should be described with that scope.

Combine a three-sentence motivation with a short This paper page, combine sample definitions with the relevant method, and put concise takeaways near tables. Preserve a requested Bayes derivation or full results table. Keep the title first; if a motivating paradox is used, place it after the title. This example illustrates decisions; it is not a required topic or mandatory page allocation.
